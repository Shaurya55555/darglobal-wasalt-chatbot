# Dar Global: third-party press background
Source: third-party press coverage (Zawya, MarketScreener, AGBI, Construction Digital, AJ Bell, Alliance News). Where this conflicts with the company's own site (darglobal_site.md and others), prefer the company site.

## Company profile
- Dar Global PLC is a luxury real estate developer, founded in 2017 as the international arm of Saudi developer Dar Al Arkan. Dar Al Arkan is the major shareholder (one source reports about 88% as of March 2024).
- Dar Global is listed on the London Stock Exchange (ticker DAR). The listing completed in February 2023 at a valuation of about SAR 2.26 billion (USD 600 million). Dar Al Arkan describes it as the first homegrown Saudi brand listed on the LSE.
- It develops residential, hospitality and mixed-use projects outside Saudi Arabia. Press reports often say Dubai; the company's own FAQ says headquartered in London.
- It works with global luxury brands, including the Trump Organization, Pagani, Aston Martin, Lamborghini, Missoni, Elie Saab and Mouawad.

## Footprint and projects (as reported)
- Markets: Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Oman, Spain, with plans to enter the US market (2025 expansion reported).
- In 2024 the company unveiled ten landmark luxury projects across Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Oman and Spain, with combined value above USD 1.9 billion.
- AIDA: project in Oman, a joint venture with Omran Group, the executive arm for Oman's tourism development.
- Tierra Viva: Spain, the company's first project in continental Europe, inspired by the design of Automobili Lamborghini.
- Les Vagues: Qatar, the first branded residential project in the country, with interiors by Elie Saab.
- A hotel and golf project in Oman tied to the Trump Organization has been reported.
- Mouawad high jewellery house: a SAR 880 million landmark residential project unveiled jointly with Dar Global.
- On its fifth anniversary the company cited about 6,100 units under construction across 16 projects.
- Three luxury developments (Tierra Viva, AIDA, Les Vagues) entered the construction phase.
