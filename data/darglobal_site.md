## About DarGlobal

## Why invest: residency and tax benefits (as stated by DarGlobal)
- Saudi Arabia: invest SAR 4 million (about USD 1.07M) to secure Saudi residency; 30-year corporate tax exemption; SAR is pegged to USD; investors can open a bank account while on a visit visa.
- Oman: lifetime residency for real estate investors; hassle-free bank account; 0% personal income tax; 0% import/export duties.
- Qatar: no personal income tax and no property tax; permanent residency by investing QAR 3,650,000 (USD 1 million); investor visa program for a minimum investment of QAR 728,000 (USD 200,000), with a Sanad Mabdayi temporary title deed issued by the Ministry of Justice so buyers can apply for residency even before completion.
- UAE: invest AED 2 million (about USD 545,000), including off-plan property, for a 10-year Golden Visa; 0% income tax; 0% property tax.
- Featured markets: Ras Al Khaimah, Muscat, Costa del Sol, Doha, London, Dubai, Riyadh and Jeddah.

## Press: Jeddah development valued at SAR 1 billion (16 Nov 2026)
Dar Global announced a new mixed-use development in Jeddah with an estimated GDV of about SAR 1 billion (USD 267 million), on land acquired for about USD 56 million. It is part of a programme to expand its Saudi portfolio by up to SAR 4 billion, taking the global pipeline to about USD 24 billion once complete. It will introduce a new international luxury brand to Jeddah through an exclusive co-branded residential concept. Existing Jeddah developments include Trump Plaza and Padel Living. Buyers from 61 nationalities are investing in the Saudi portfolio.

## Press: Dar Global Capital Partners (26 Oct 2026)
Dar Global Capital Partners (DGCP) is the alternative asset management arm of Dar Global. It targets a cross-border mandate across the GCC, Europe, the UK and the US, with high-conviction investments in Dubai, operating from the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) under Dubai Financial Services Authority (DFSA) oversight, and is exploring a Dubai-focused real estate opportunities fund. Ziad El Chaar, CEO of Dar Global, was appointed Chairman of DGCP. Dar Global recently upgraded to the Equity Shares (Commercial Companies) category on the London Stock Exchange and serves investors from more than 125 nationalities.

## Press: Trump Villas Simaisma, Qatar (7 Oct 2026)
Sales opened for Trump Villas within Trump International Golf Club Simaisma, Qatar, after strong demand since 1 October. Three- and five-bedroom villas in a gated community, views of the 18-hole golf course, Simaisma beach and the sea. Prices start from QAR 5 million. Site works are expected to begin in early 2027 with completion targeted for the end of 2030. It will be Doha's first gated golf community with a community centre (padel and tennis courts) and a members-only golf and beach club. 100% freehold ownership.

## Press: Trump International Hotel & Tower Dubai contractor (6 Aug 2026)

The tower is on Sheikh Zayed Road at the gateway to Downtown Dubai, introduced in April 2025, about 350 metres and 80 floors, with hospitality, branded residences and a private members' club, Dubai's highest outdoor swimming pool, and two signature penthouses inspired by Trump Tower on Fifth Avenue.
