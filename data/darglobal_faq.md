## FAQ: who and where
- DarGlobal is headquartered in London and operates across 14 cities in 9 countries, including Spain, the UK, UAE, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Greece and the Maldives. It is publicly listed (London Stock Exchange).
- It has over three decades of development experience and offers residences, cities and hospitality projects.
- Locations named: Dubai, Ras Al Khaimah, Muscat, Doha, Riyadh, Jeddah, Costa del Sol (Spain), the Maldives, London. Sales hubs for clients in Dubai, Riyadh, Jeddah, Muscat, Doha, London, Marbella and New York.
- Open for investment: multiple developments across the UAE, Oman, KSA, Spain, London and the Maldives, including branded districts and resort-led communities.

## FAQ: ROI, ROE and ROL
DarGlobal says investors value ROI (Return on Investment: financial growth and capital appreciation), ROE (Return on Ego: pride, identity and social value of owning something distinctive) and ROL (Return on Legacy: confidence that what you build today will stand tomorrow).

## FAQ: buying process and payment
- Process: 1) choose your property, 2) review the investment plan with advisors, 3) reserve your unit securely, 4) sign digitally or through offices worldwide, 5) complete payment and registration with support.
- International buyers can invest, with full ownership rights, residency privileges where applicable, and a digital process for remote investors.
- Payment plans: each development offers flexible installment plans. Off-plan means early entry, attractive prices and flexible payment plans.
- Registration is done with the land department or authority of each country. Typical documents: passport copy, contact information, reservation form, payment details (some markets need more).
- Many branded residences are offered fully furnished; select projects offer customization.
- Returns: projects are positioned for capital appreciation and rental yields. DarGlobal states investments are protected by local regulations, escrow systems and partnerships. It does not publish a guaranteed return figure.
- Co-branded residences are homes made with luxury brands (fashion, hospitality, design); DarGlobal says brand equity drives desirability, rental performance, resale value and appreciation.

## Tokenization
DarGlobal offers blockchain-powered tokenization: physical assets turned into digital tokens (fractional shares of premium properties) that investors can buy, sell or hold. Benefits stated: simplified access from anywhere, liquidity through regulated platforms backed by real-world assets, security and transparency on a blockchain, and fractional ownership of luxury residences. It positions this as enhancing liquidity, not crypto hype.
