## AIDA, Muscat, Oman: overview
AIDA is a master-planned gated community and golfing destination in Muscat, Oman, 130 metres above sea level on hilltops over the shore, designed as "a city within a city". Developed by DarGlobal in partnership with OMRAN, with brands including the Trump Organization and Marriott. About 25 minutes from Muscat International Airport (another page says 10 minutes from Downtown Muscat) and about a 60-minute flight from Dubai. Features: 18-hole Trump championship golf course (Trump International Golf Club Oman), AIDA Residence Club, hanging suites, Trump members-only club, SHFT night club, AIDA beach, five-star dining, parks, hiking and cycling trails, community and retail centre, wellness centres. Property types: villas, apartments, hotel rooms; branded and non-branded residences. Views: sea, golf course or valley/wadi. First phase of AIDA completes in 2027. Sales for AIDA were launched on 22 March 2023.

## AIDA residence collections
- Trump Golf Villas: on the frontline of Trump International Golf Club Oman; owners get a golf club membership and an exclusive branded golf cart.
- Trump Cliff Villas: exclusive 3-bedroom residences on the cliffside next to the Trump Hotel with sea views.
- Azure Oceanfront Villas, interiors by Fendi Casa: limited five-bedroom residences suspended 130 metres above the sea (19 mansions, with beach access, pool and gym).
- Coastal Investment Villas: villas close to the sea, positioned as an investment.
- Sunrise Haven Luxury Villas: fully furnished, ready for personal use or vacation rental (pre-qualified Airbnb homes).
- Fairway Villas: on the frontline of the Trump golf club, with golf membership and branded cart.
- Amour Sans Detour and Amour Sans Detour 2: villas steps from the sea and moments from the golf course, with rental management available.
- Also in AIDA: Marriott Residences and Nickelodeon Hotels & Resorts.
Golf access is through membership at Trump International Golf Club Oman, subject to application and approval.

## Why invest in Oman (as stated by DarGlobal)
Lifetime residency visa for investors in projects in the Integrated Tourism Complex (ITC); 100% freehold ownership for all nationalities in designated zones; 0% personal income tax; 0% capital gains tax; 0% inheritance tax (owner's country laws apply); 25-year corporate income tax exemption in special economic and free zones; upcoming UAE-Muscat railway; Saudi-Oman highway; tourism projected at USD 22.5 billion by 2040; over 100 nationalities in the community; Numbeo Quality of Life Index 2025 ranked Oman 4th globally and 1st in Asia and the Arab world; mortgage program for resident and non-resident buyers.

## Trump Organization partnership
The partnership spans more than a decade across AIDA (Oman), Trump Tower Jeddah (Saudi Arabia) and Trump International Hotel & Tower Dubai (UAE). Projects: The Trump Penthouses (Dubai), Trump International Hotel & Tower Dubai, Trump Tower Jeddah, Trump Cliff Villas, Trump International Hotel Muscat, Trump Golf Villas, Trump Plaza Jeddah, Rayana (Trump Mansions, Riyadh), Trump International Simaisma (Qatar).
- Trump International Oman at AIDA: a USD 500 million, 140-key, five-star hotel complex, scheduled to open in December 2028; launched in June 2024.
- Trump Tower Jeddah: SAR 2 billion (about USD 531 million), 47 floors on the Jeddah Corniche, launched in December 2024; includes the Trump Club, Saudi Arabia's first members-only social club. Main works contract awarded to Arabian Construction Company (Nov 2025) after enabling and foundation works (SIFCO did enabling works in Feb 2025).
- Trump Plaza Jeddah: launched January 2026, valued at over USD 1 billion, the third collaboration with the Trump Organization in Saudi Arabia, within Amaya along King Abdulaziz Road. Main works contractor: Al Dahlawi Company for Contracting (April 2026). Amaya is a 1 million sq m integrated community called Jeddah's "Beverly Hills" in the making.
- Trump International Hotel & Tower Dubai: launched 30 April 2025, 80 floors, Sheikh Zayed Road; includes "The Trump" members-only club. Enabling works contract awarded to Edrafor Emirates in December 2025.
- Trump International Golf Club Doha and beachfront villas in Simaisma, Qatar: announced 30 April 2025.
- Rayana, Wadi Safar, Riyadh: SAR 338 million infrastructure contract awarded to Compass and Bin Omairah Company for Contracting (18 June 2026).
- History: in November 2022 DarGlobal and the Trump Organization signed an agreement in New York for a signature Trump resort in AIDA (villas, hotel, golf course).
